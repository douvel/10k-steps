FAQ

Does it cost anything?

No, the app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where do my steps come from?

Apple Health, no manual entry. Walkever reads your steps read-only, from iPhone alone or with an Apple Watch.

Can I change my goal?

Yes, 10,000 steps a day by default; the monthly goal is calculated for you from the number of days in the month.

What is the required average?

The number of steps per day you still need to hit your monthly goal, with or without today. You can also try it with our online calculator.

Is my data collected?

No. Nothing leaves your iPhone: no account, no ads, no tracking.

Which languages?

English and French.
