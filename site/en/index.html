Frequently asked questions

Is the app really free?

Yes. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

What data does it use?

Only your steps, read from Apple Health with your permission, read-only. They never leave your iPhone and nothing is collected.

Can I change my 10,000-step goal?

Yes. Pick a quick goal (5,000 to 15,000 steps) or enter your own. The monthly goal is calculated for you.

Is it available on Android?

Not yet: the app is currently available on iPhone only.

Which languages does it support?

English and French, depending on your iPhone's language.

Ready to keep your pace?

Install the app and see right away where you stand this month.
