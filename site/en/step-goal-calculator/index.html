How many steps a day to hit your monthly goal?

Enter your daily goal and the steps you have done this month: the calculator gives you the daily average you need to finish the month on track, and how far ahead or behind the ideal pace you are.

How it is calculated

  • Monthly goal = daily goal × days in the month (310,000 steps in October at 10,000/day, 300,000 in November).
  • Required average = steps left ÷ days left. Days left include today, unless you choose to start tomorrow.
  • Ahead or behind = steps done − daily goal × days already elapsed. At 95,000 steps on the morning of the 10th, you are 5,000 steps ahead of a 10,000/day goal.

Why think in months

A fixed daily goal has one flaw: miss one day and the streak is gone. A monthly goal keeps the same bar (the same average) with more flexibility: a 4,000-step office day is made up by a Sunday hike. You also see right away whether you are seriously behind or not: 300 steps a day to catch up is three minutes of walking.

FAQ

What monthly step goal should I set?

Multiply your daily goal by the days in the month. At 8,000 steps a day that is 240,000 steps in November and 248,000 in December.

Should today count in the days left?

In the morning, yes: you still have the day ahead. In the evening, exclude today to see what you need from tomorrow on.

How many steps a month is 10,000 a day?

Between 280,000 (February) and 310,000 steps (31-day months). Over a year that is 3,650,000 steps.

Does the calculator store my data?

No. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
