Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Walkever app (the "app"), developed by BELLASOR.

Data collected

The app does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on external servers. All data stays on your device.

Health data (Apple HealthKit and Android Health Connect)

With your explicit permission, the app reads your step data through Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Health Connect (Android, step-count read permission only) to show your daily and monthly progress. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never shared with third parties
  • Is not used for advertising or analytics
  • Is not modified — the app is read-only

You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices (iOS) or Health Connect → App permissions (Android).

Local preferences

Your daily step goal is saved locally on your device using the app's internal storage. It is not transmitted.

Ads and tracking

The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics tools.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information about them.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
