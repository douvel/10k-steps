Why Walkever if you want to track a monthly step goal

Apple Health tells you how many steps you have taken. Walkever tells you whether that is enough. The app calculates in real time how many steps a day you still need to reach your goal by the end of the month — with or without today — and shows your cumulative lead or lag. No manual entry, no account required, no data leaving your iPhone.

Is Apple Health enough?

For basic counting, yes. Apple Health uses the iPhone accelerometer and shows your steps under Health › Activity. It displays a weekly trend and a 7-day average. What it does not do: calculate the daily average you need to finish your month on track, or show how far ahead or behind your ideal pace you are.

FAQ

What is the best free step counter app for iPhone?

Walkever if you want to track a monthly goal with no ads and no data collection. Apple Health if you just want automatic step counting built into iOS. Both read HealthKit and are completely free.

Which pedometer app does not collect my data?

Walkever and Apple Health send no data to external servers — everything stays on your iPhone. Check each app's privacy policy before installing.

Can I change my step goal on iPhone?

Yes. In Walkever you set your goal directly in the app. In Apple Health your activity goal is in the Summary tab. Both let you customise the target.

Does Apple Health count steps automatically?

Yes. Apple Health uses the iPhone accelerometer to count steps continuously with no setup. The data is in Health › Activity › Steps.
